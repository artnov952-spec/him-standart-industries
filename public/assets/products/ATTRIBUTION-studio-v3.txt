HIM STANDART INDUSTRIES: product illustration attribution, current display version studio-v3
Updated: 2026-10-30

These are public-source illustrations of substances or physical forms, not photographs of products or batches supplied by HIM STANDART INDUSTRIES. The 29 physical-form fallbacks remain labeled as generic illustrations in the catalogue.

Four newly displayed images were processed with the built-in ImageGen tool: background replacement and reframing on a warm near-white square canvas, using the cited real photograph as the edit target. They are source-derived, generatively processed illustrations. Pixel identity to the original is not claimed. No commercial labels, percentages, manufacturing dates or supplier branding were added. Source author and license are retained; CC BY-SA derivatives are offered under the same respective CC BY-SA license.

Technical export: generated PNG to WebP quality 85 using Sharp, without resize, crop or image filters. All four WebP files are 1254 x 1254 pixels. Source JPEGs and PNG masters are kept in outputs/photo-art-direction-masters-v3 (excluded from Git); source files in public are preserved.

Other currently displayed illustrations retain the authors and licenses below. Their earlier background processing is documented in ATTRIBUTION-studio-v2.txt and IMAGE-SOURCES.md. Acetone and iron-chloride-hexahydrate retain their original neutral photographs without a raster background edit. No endorsement by any source author is implied.
